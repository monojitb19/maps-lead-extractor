# Maps Lead Extractor

**Search Google Maps. Click once. Get a ready-to-use lead list.**

Collects business names, phone numbers, emails, websites and social media links from Google Maps, and downloads everything as a spreadsheet.

</div>

---

## ✨ What you get

For every business in your search:

- 🏢 **Business name and category**
- 📞 **Phone number**, from Google Maps and from their website
- ✉️ **Email address**, found on their website
- 🌐 **Website and full address**
- 🔗 **LinkedIn, Facebook and X (Twitter) pages**
- ⭐ **Rating and number of reviews**
- 📍 **Google Maps link and location**

Everything downloads as one **CSV file** that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

## 🚀 Setup (one time, 2 minutes)

1. Download this folder and unzip it.
2. Open Chrome and type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (switch in the top right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Click the 🧩 puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and **pin** Maps Lead Extractor.

When Chrome asks for permission, click **Allow**. The extension needs it to visit business websites and find emails.

## 📋 How to use

**1. Search**
Click the extension icon, type what you're looking for, and press Enter.
*Example: `steel traders Howrah`*

**2. Start**
Once the results appear on Google Maps, click **Start Auto Extract**.
Sit back. It scrolls through all the results and collects the details on its own. You can see the count go up live.

**3. Wait for emails**
After the listings are done, it keeps looking for emails in the background. Wait until the message *"Finding emails…"* disappears.

**4. Download**
Click **Export Leads** to download your spreadsheet.

**5. Start fresh**
Click **Clear** when you want to begin a new list.

> 💡 You can close the popup while it's working. It keeps going.
> Need to stop midway? Click **Stop Extract**. Nothing collected so far is lost.

## 💡 Tips for bigger, better lists

**Search small areas, not whole cities.**
Google Maps shows only about 120 results per search. Instead of `steel traders Kolkata`, run several smaller searches:

- `steel fabricators saudi arabia`
- `rebar importers vioetnam`

All results add up in one list, and duplicates are removed automatically.

**Keep the Chrome window full size** while it's running.

**Let it run at its own pace.** It pauses briefly between businesses on purpose, so Google doesn't block you.

## ❓ Common questions

**Why are some emails or social links empty?**
They're only filled in when the business lists them on its own website. Many businesses don't have a website, or don't show an email on it. The extension leaves the box empty rather than guessing, so everything you get is real.

**Why are there two phone columns?**
*Phone* is the number on Google Maps. *Website Phone* shows extra numbers found on the company's own website, often direct mobile numbers.

**Does it open lots of tabs?**
No. Websites are checked quietly in the background. You'll only see Google Maps.

**Is my data sent anywhere?**
No. Everything stays on your computer until you download it.

**It says "No results list found".**
Search something on Google Maps first and wait for the list of businesses to appear on the left, then click Start.

**It suddenly stopped collecting names or phone numbers.**
Google occasionally changes how Maps looks. Contact whoever set up the extension for an update.
